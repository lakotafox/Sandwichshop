Our Story

Portland's Favorite Vegan Spot

What started as a beloved food cart in downtown Portland has grown into SE Division's go-to destination for incredible vegan comfort food.

Our mission is simple: prove that plant-based food can be just as satisfying and delicious as anything else. From our famous Steak & Cheese to our crispy Cajun Po'Boy, every item is crafted with care.

🌱

Our Promise: Despite the words on the menu, we do not serve meat, cheese, eggs, or dairy. Everything is 100% vegan!
