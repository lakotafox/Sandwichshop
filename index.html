About the data

Every clip on this site comes live from the open API of Archive Genocide, which preserves footage from Gaza and the West Bank posted by journalists, channels and residents since October 2023. Nothing is stored or altered here.

Captions come from the people who posted the footage, not from this site. A record shows what was posted and when. It is a primary source to examine, not a finding. The date is when the source posted the clip, which is not always when the event happened.

The footage belongs to the people who filmed it. Please credit them and Archive Genocide when you share it, and respect the people shown.
