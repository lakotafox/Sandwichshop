III. Notes

About the data

  1. 01

    Where it comes from

    Every clip loads live from the open API of Archive Genocide, which preserves footage posted by journalists, channels and residents. Nothing is stored or altered here.

  2. 02

    Captions and dates

    Captions are written by the people who posted the footage, not by this site. The date is when the source posted the clip, which is not always when the event happened.

  3. 03

    The review mark

    High, medium or low shows how closely the archive’s review matches the clip to what it documents. It reflects review status, not a judgement about whether the event happened.

  4. 04

    Reading a location

    Town and neighbourhood pins mark the centre of an area named in the caption. Building means a named hospital or school; the footage may be from nearby. Exact site is geolocated from the footage or from cited reporting.

  5. 05

    Who the footage belongs to

    The people who filmed it. Credit them and Archive Genocide when you share it, and respect the people shown.
