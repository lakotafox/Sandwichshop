Our Story

Portland's Favorite Vegan Spot

DC Vegetarian has been serving Portland's SE Division Street with incredible 100% vegan comfort food. Our mission is simple: prove that plant-based food can be just as satisfying and delicious as anything else.

From our famous submarine sandwiches to our mouthwatering burgers, every item is crafted with care using the freshest ingredients. No meat, no cheese, no eggs, no dairy - just pure deliciousness!
